Sand Flow Coefficient Determination – NF P18-564
Overview
This repository documents a laboratory report on the determination of the sand flow coefficient (permeability) in accordance with the French standard NF P18-564. The work was conducted as part of the Master 1 RMGE (Mineral Resources, Geomaterials, and Environment) program at the University of Mohammed Seddik Ben Yahia – Jijel, Faculty of Natural and Life Sciences, Department of Earth and Universe Sciences, during the 2023/2024 academic year.

Standard Reference
NF P18-564: French standard for determining the flow coefficient of sand

Purpose of the Test
The objective of this test is to measure the flow capacity (permeability) of sand. This information is essential for:

Evaluating sand quality for construction projects

Ensuring proper groundwater management

Assessing suitability for civil engineering applications

Verifying compliance with quality and safety standards

Field of Application
This standard applies to the determination of the sand flow coefficient used in various fields, including:

Construction

Civil engineering

Landscape architecture

Geotechnical investigations

Groundwater management

Definitions
Key terms for understanding the test procedure:

Term	Definition
Flow Coefficient (k)	A measure of sand's permeability – its ability to allow water to pass through
Representative Sample	A sample that accurately reflects the characteristics of the bulk material
Test Tube	The cylindrical container used to hold the sand sample during testing
Flow Rate	The volume of water passing through the sand sample per unit time
Test Principle
The test consists of measuring the flow rate of water through a prepared sand sample placed in a test tube. The flow rate is influenced by:

Sand permeability (intrinsic property)

Grain size distribution (granulometry)

Applied pressure (hydraulic head)

The principle is based on:

Precise measurement of the flow rate

Application of specific formulas to calculate the flow coefficient (k)

Apparatus Required
Equipment	Purpose
Test tube	Cylindrical container for the sand sample
Water source with pump	Supplies water for the test
Flow measurement device	Measures the volume of water passing through
Pressure gauges (manometers)	Measures hydraulic pressure
Stopwatch/chronometer	Times the flow duration
Safety accessories	Ensures safe operation
Sample Preparation
The preparation of the sand sample for testing involves:

Sampling: Obtaining a representative sample of the sand to be tested

Drying: Removing excess moisture from the sample

Sieving: Ensuring the sample meets the required granulometric specifications

Compaction: Packing the sand into the test tube according to standard procedures

Test Execution
The test is carried out in the following sequence:

Placement: The prepared sand sample is placed in the test tube

Water supply: Water is introduced from the source through the pump

Flow measurement: The flow rate is measured using the flow measurement device

Pressure recording: Pressure is monitored using manometers

Timing: The duration of flow is recorded with a chronometer

Data collection: Multiple measurements are taken for accuracy

Expression of Results
The test results are expressed as the sand flow coefficient (k) , which indicates the sand's ability to transmit water.

Factors affecting the coefficient:

Grain size distribution

Applied pressure

Sand density

Porosity

Compaction degree

Interpretation:

Higher k values indicate higher permeability (coarser sand)

Lower k values indicate lower permeability (finer sand)

Results must be compared with reference standards to evaluate sand quality

Typical applications of the coefficient:

Drainage system design

Filter layer selection

Groundwater flow modeling

Dam and embankment design
